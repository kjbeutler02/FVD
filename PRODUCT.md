# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Authorized Strong & Hanni law firm personnel — attorneys, paralegals, and staff who need to pull the complete document set for a matter out of Filevine. They arrive with a project ID (or a Filevine project URL) and a job to finish: get every relevant document onto their machine as an organized ZIP. They are not power users of this tool; they use it occasionally, under time pressure, often mid-task on a case.

## Product Purpose

A single-purpose internal utility: authenticate, open a Filevine project, browse its folder structure, choose what to pull, and download it as a folder-structured ZIP. Download-only — no uploading, editing, or syncing. Success is a staffer who lands, recognizes the firm's brand, finds the folders they need without instruction, and walks away with the right ZIP on the first try.

## Brand Personality

Voice: clean, formal, professional, credible. Three words: **Noticed, Believed, Remembered** (the firm's own marketing philosophy). The interface should feel like it belongs to an established law firm — restrained, trustworthy, unfussy. No slang, no jokes, no exclamation marks. Copy reinforces authority ("Authorized firm personnel only") over friendliness ("Welcome!").

## Anti-references

- Consumer-playful file apps (bright multi-color folders, cartoon illustrations, emoji).
- Gimmicky effects: glassmorphism, heavy drop shadows, decorative gradients, glows, animated confetti.
- SaaS-cliché hero-metric dashboards and tiny uppercase tracked eyebrows on every block.
- Anything that competes with or recolors the firm wordmark.

## Design Principles

1. **Earned familiarity.** Borrow the proven file-browser model (Google Drive / Dropbox / OneDrive) so users navigate without learning anything. The tool disappears into the task.
2. **Lawyer-appropriate restraint.** One brand color (deep blue), generous whitespace, hairline borders, at most a light shadow. Restraint is the brand.
3. **The wordmark is the identity.** No symbol, no second logo. The deep-blue band and the STRONG & HANNI lockup carry recognition.
4. **Honest about the model.** Selecting a folder downloads everything inside it; the UI must make that consequence legible before the click.
5. **Every state designed.** Loading (scan), empty folders, errors, partial-failure downloads — all branded and explained, never a dead end.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA: body text ≥4.5:1, large/UI text ≥3:1 against its background; brand-blue focus rings on every interactive element; full keyboard operability for the folder browser, selection, and download.
- `prefers-reduced-motion`: drawer/reveal transitions degrade to instant or crossfade.
- Selection state never conveyed by color alone (checkbox glyph + tint + border together).
